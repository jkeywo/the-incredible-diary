reduced the deckchairs to approximately half their earlier size. Both missions
use `07_crew_cabins.png` at the same scale; Mission 1 blacks out the entire upper
cabin section, including ceiling and dividing pillars, and adds closed leaves.
The earlier `06_passage_revised.png` is an unused draft.
Foreground frame overlays preserve the original brass artwork above characters.
The vent sits slightly left of the northern steam-room entrance and blows right;
the steam, rather than the pipe, blocks the doorway during Mission 1's death scene.
