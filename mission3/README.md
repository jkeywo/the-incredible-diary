# Mission 3 — Landing in Greece

An exploration leg following Mission 2. The complete ship, its cabin doors,
characters and six-hour clock remain available. The Greek dock replaces the
previous port, with a restaurant to its left and a market to its right.
The restaurant has four dining tables and an accessible kitchen at the back.
The market has five stalls in an arc and two smaller stalls in the middle.

Launch from **Dev Menu → Mission 3 — Landing in Greece**, or turn the page at
the end of Mission 2. Dev Menu resumes an unfinished visit; turning the page
starts a fresh arrival. Saves use `user://mission3.journal`, including recorded
history. No new hazards or rescue objectives are authored for this leg.

The dock reuses Mission 1's separate ship and sea textures and motion script.
Only the fixed Greek land layer is new. Ship interiors reuse Mission 2 scenes.
